A few good questions

Wondering
how this works?

That’s healthy. Trustworthy wedding software should be understandable.

Is Grace replacing our wedding website?+

Grace includes the guest-facing website, but the larger product is your shared planning room and structured wedding system. The website is one beautiful view of the same approved information.

Can Grace message guests by herself?+

No. Sending invitations or updates is a consequential action and requires explicit approval immediately before anything is sent.

Does one partner control the account?+

No. Both partners are equal members by default. Individual preferences stay attributed and a joint decision requires genuine agreement unless you deliberately assign a decision owner.

Is this a full Zola replacement today?+

Not yet. The prototype is focused on the magical core: a shared room, guest and event data, a live website, RSVPs, confirmations, and registry links. Commerce and fulfillment are intentionally outside the first version.

When will pricing be available?+

After the early design-partner phase. We will publish clear pricing only once the product scope and support model have been validated with real couples.
